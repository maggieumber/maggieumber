Chicago Printers Guild Turns Sweet 16! Join us under the iconic Tiffany Dome at the Chicago Cultural Center on Saturday, October 25, 2025, as we mark 16 years of the Chicago Printers Guild with the 7th Annual CPG Publishers Fair. This isn’t just a print fair—it’s a one-day creative takeover celebrating the Midwest’s vibrant print culture.
Expect bold prints, zines, posters, and artist books alongside live DJ sets, hands-on printmaking, and real talk with today’s printmakers. From DIY to fine art, analog to experimental, CPG invites the whole city to vibe, explore, and celebrate the living legacy of print in the heart of Chicago.
