Henderson Hummingbird Hurrah
Sat, 8/16, 9am-4pm
Bender Park
200 N 3rd St
Henderson, MN 56044

The 17th Henderson Hummingbird Hurrah is a one-day annual event sponsored by Henderson Feathers (nonprofit 501c3) to celebrate the Ruby-throated Hummingbird. Free events will promote the understanding, appreciation, and enjoyment of the Ruby-throated Hummingbird for people of all ages. There will also be speakers, hummingbird banding, Hummingbird Garden Tour, educational displays, food vendors, craft vendors, children’s activities, raffle prizes located in Bender Park and in the Henderson Hummingbird Garden . . . watch for signs!!
