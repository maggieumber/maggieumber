Rust Belt Review vol. 6 was up for an Ignatz for Outstanding Anthology! One of my Chrysanthemum Under the Waves comics is in there amongst many gems.

Cartoonist Laura Terry presented the award for Outstanding Anthology at the Small Press Expo award ceremony. Here she is announcing that our anthology won! Cartoonist and contributor Andrew Greenstone accepted the award on behalf of editor and publisher Sean Knickerbocker. You can watch the whole award show on SPX's YouTube channel.
