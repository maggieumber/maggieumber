Mark your calendars! On 1/24 I'll be leading SAW's Free Friday Night Workshop on the theme of demon lovers.

Speaking of demons... Chrysanthemum Under the Waves was included on a few more 2024 best of year lists! It appeared on Lars Ingebrigtsen's Random Thoughts The best comics of 2024. Rick Ray on Goodreads placed it on his Best Comics of 2024 shelf. It was number four on Ryan C.'s Four Color Apocalypse Top 10 Collected Editions - Contemporary.

Ryan writes:

Lush, lurid, decadent, and deadly, Umber has crafted the strongest horror comics statement of the decade, if not longer, with this, her most dramatic and confident work to date. It's impossible to pick a favorite among these compelling and captivating stories, so why even try? Just enjoy them all. Follow the siren call. Your demon lover awaits.
