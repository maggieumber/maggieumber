Tomorrow through Sunday I'm tabling at the Indiana Dunes Birding Festival's Migration Marketplace! I'll have my new mini-comic about sea turtles, So Many Ways, all of my owl books, a ton of new greeting cards and prints! Come find me at table 10 under the tent by the Indiana Dunes Visitor Center!

The 2025 Migration Marketplace will offer an outdoor, safe experience to shop great birding gear and merchandise. The Migration Marketplace showcases a variety of bird and nature related items, birding travel information, optics dealers, crafts, birding apparel and gear, feeders, and more!

2025 Marketplace Dates & Times
Thursday, May 15: 11am-4pm
Friday, May 16: 11am-4pm
Saturday, May 17: 11am-4pm
Sunday, May 18: 11am-2pm
