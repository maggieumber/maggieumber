My mini kuš!, The Heaviness of Glowing Blue Clouds, was reviewed by Ryan C.'s Four Color Apocalypse! Here are some excerpts (you can read the full review at the link below):

It's unlikely that there's anyone reading this who isn't familiar with the old cliche "the best horror happens off-screen," (or words to that effect), but tired as invoking it may be, the sentiment definitely applies to our friend Maggie Umber's latest, THE HEAVINESS OF GLOWING BLUE CLOUDS (issue #148 in the indefatigable MINI KUS! range --- and did you all notice that they got Renee French to do #150? But that's another matter for another time), albeit with one important caveat : I'm not entirely sure that it's a horror comic.

{% include blog-double.html %}

I mentioned a moment ago about understanding being just out of reach here, and I actually ENJOYED and APPECIATED the fact that it was. I don't for one moment doubt that Umber has a hard-and-fast POINT in mind when she made this, but I rather relished not knowing what it is and just letting this comic wash over me, pass through me, and leave a lingering sensation of otherworldliness afterwards. I used the word "conjured" earlier with precision --- there's something very akin to magic happening in these pages.
