Pit Bull Eyes debuts at Chicago Zine Fest on Saturday! I got very excited when I was asked to table at the fest and read at the fundraiser. So I decided to paint a brand new dream (nightmare?) comic in red and green watercolors about a girl and a pit bull. If you'd like to say hi and pick it up at the fest, here's the info:

Saturday, July 19, 2025, 11 am to 4 pm
Harold Washington Library Center
Winter Garden, 9th Floor
400 S State St
Chicago, IL 60605
