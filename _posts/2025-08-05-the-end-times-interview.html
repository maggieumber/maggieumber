I was interviewed by The END PRESS for THE END Times Issue 2: “It’s hotter than hell” the ecology issue. The interview was conducted via email letters over a six months time span. We wrote about creativity, nature, Shirley Jackson, and Chrysanthemum Under the Waves. Also features work by @eddyleonelaldana of @otravezpress and @museum_of_infinite_outcomes. Debuted originally at Chicago Zine Fest in summer 2025. Pick up a copy the next time you come across The END PRESS at a show!
