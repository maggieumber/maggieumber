I've been self-publishing for 3 years! From 2023-2025, I self-published 10 mini-comics and 1 graphic novel, and I contributed to 7 magazines. In support of the books, I did 54 tour stops. These were zine and birding fests, comic cons, exhibition spaces, reading events, and virtual classrooms. The books got a lot of great press, awards, and reviews. Thank you to everyone for your tremendous support and encouragement! I'm so grateful to you all.
