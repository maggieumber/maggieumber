MAC FEST (MADISON ALTERNATIVE COMICS FEST)
Sat, December 13th, 2025.
1226 Williamson St. 53703. Madison. WI.
11:AM - 6:PM

Special guest:
MAGGIE UMBER
Maggie Umber is a cartoonist and publisher based in Chicago, Il. In addition to her own work she co-founded the comics publisher 2-Cloud. Maggie is a recent Bram Stoker Awards Nominee and her recent self-published graphic novel Chrysanthemum Under the Waves was excerpted in the Ignatz winning Rust Belt Review Vol 6. Maggie is a festival stalwart, having tabled at 20+ festivals in 2025. We’re so excited to read her newest book The GOK Specials which will be The GOK Specials which will be available at MAC. Thank you for joining us Maggie!
