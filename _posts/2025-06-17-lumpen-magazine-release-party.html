I'm in the new Lumpen Magazine Comics issue! I hope to have copies at CAKE (Chicago Alternative Comics Expo), CZF (Chicago Zine Fest), South Side Zine Fest, and INZF (Insert Name Zine Fest). Here's the release party info:

Looking for a fun weekend hangout? Look no further! Lumpen Comics Issue #143 is officially out after a three-year hiatus, and we’re celebrating at Co-prosperity! 🎉🎉🎉

Join us Friday, June 20th from 7pm-10pm for the Lumpen Comics Release Party! Music, drinks, and fun await! Can’t wait to see you there!

Lumpen Comics Issue #143 is a special issue featuring contributions from Kansas City-based and Chicago-based artists! Come check out what they’ve made in this new release–and be sure to take a peek at their other artistic endeavors!
