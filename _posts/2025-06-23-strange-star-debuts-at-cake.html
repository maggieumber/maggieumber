Strange Star
12 pgs
8.5 in x 11 in, folds out to 121 in
Inkjet accordion book, matte cardstock cover

Summary: On August 6, 1797, astronomer Carolina Herschel discovered the comet that heralded the birth of Mary Shelley on August 30, 1797. Carolina was the world's first professional female astronomer. She assisted her brother, William Herschel, in his careers in astronomy and music. Mary Shelley went on to write Frankenstein.
