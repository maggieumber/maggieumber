I'll have all 4 of my 2025 zines (So Many Ways, Strange Star, Pit Bull Eyes, Bunworld 2) this Saturday at the South Side Zine Fest! I love this library and am excited to be a part of this show's first year! Thank you so much to the organizers and volunteers for all of your hard work. I hope to see you there!

Join us for South Side Zine Fest at 11 a.m.-4 p.m. Saturday, July 26 at the Richard J. Daley Branch of the Chicago Public Library, 3400 S. Halsted St. in Bridgeport. Free! More info at zineclubchicago.com.
