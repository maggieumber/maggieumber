This Friday evening, I'll be at the opening for the Marathon Art Show at Fantagraphics Bookstore & Gallery in Seattle, WA. I'll have a watercolor from Chrysanthemum Under the Waves hanging in the show.

Saturday, I'll be tabling at the Short Run Comix Festival in Seattle, WA. Come find me among this incredible group of cartoonists! If you're in the area, I hope to see you at both events!

Festival and exhibit details below:

Our 12th annual fest is ONE WEEK AWAY! Visit shortrun.org for info on all our programming the day of and for the whole weekend. Check out the table map, now up on the site, and make sure you don’t miss our special guests and all your favorite artists!

Short Run Comix Festival
Fisher Pavilion at Seattle Center
Saturday, November 2nd, 11 am – 6 pm.
FREE admission.
